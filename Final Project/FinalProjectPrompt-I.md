# Final Project Prompt: Fundamental

## Basic Details

**Genre:** Sci-Fi or Fantasy

**Game Engine:** Unity

**Build Platform:** Windows & Mac

**Size:** 5 GB or Below

## Prompt

**✧** Create a small game following the details noted below while using assets from **Unity Game Development Club's** asset library or **Unity Asset Store**.

**> Gameplay Requirements** 

The game must have:

- At least 10 minutes of gameplay
- Clear beginning, middle, and ending
- Main menu
- Pause menu
- Settings menu
- Win condition
- Lose condition
- Credits

**> Boss Requirements** 

The boss must have:

- At least 3 unique attacks
- Unique animations (Must use them)
- Have an obvious weakness or strategy
- Trigger an ending upon defeat

**> Parkour Requirements** 

The map must include:

- Precision jumping
- Moving platforms

**> Weapon Requirements** 

The weapon must include:

- Primary attack
- Sound effects
- Visual effects

**> Map Requirements** 

The map must contain:

- Spawn area
- Exploration area
- Parkour section
- Boss arena
- Ending area

**> Art Style**

Maintain one consistent art style.

Do not mix:

- Pixel art
- Stylized
- Photorealistic

**Lighting should remain consistent throughout the project.**

**> Required Systems**

- Player health
- Enemy AI
- Checkpoint or respawn system
- Scene transitions
- Audio manager
- Settings menu

**> User Interface**

Required:

- Health Bar
- Crosshair (if applicable)
- Pause Menu
- Main Menu
- Settings Menu
- Credits

**> Audio**

Include:

- Background music
- Ambient sounds
- Footsteps
- Weapon sounds
- Enemy sounds
- Boss music

**> Polish**

Required:
- Screen transitions
- Particle effects
- Camera shake
- UI animations
- Loading screen

**> Technical Requirements**

- No game-breaking bugs
- Save system not required
- Keyboard & mouse support

**> Performance**

The final build should:
- Launch without errors
- Maintain smooth gameplay
- Complete without crashes
- Avoid major visual or audio glitches

**> Aditional Features**

You may choose up to 10 bonus features for your game.

#### [Bonus Features](https://github.com/Nox-Erebos/Introduction-To-Unity-Course-Documentation/blob/main/Final-Project/Bonus-Features.md)

## Project Details

**Time Limit:** 6 Months

**> Submission**

Submit:
- Unity Project
- Final Build
- Source Code
- README
- Credits
- Gameplay Trailer (1–3 minutes)

**> Credits**

All third-party assets must be credited.

Include:
- Asset Store assets
- Audio
- Fonts
- Models
- Textures

**Record Credits inside the README**

#### [README TEMPLATE](https://github.com/Nox-Erebos/Introduction-To-Unity-Course-Documentation/blob/main/Final-Project/README-Template.md)

**> Restrictions**

- Club Members may use AI to assist them in debugging. **ONLY AFTER YOU EXHAUST ALL OTHER METHODS OF HELP**
- Asset Store assets may be used.
- Club assets may be modified.
- No copyrighted content.
- No unfinished placeholder assets in the final build.

**> Scope Rules**

Required Features:
- Everything listed under Prompt.

Adding more weapons, maps, mechanics, or systems will not increase the project's evaluation score if required features are incomplete.

You can optionally add a story. **Do not feel pressured to make a story!**

### Focus on completing a polished, enjoyable experience within the project scope. A finished, stable game is valued more highly than an ambitious but incomplete one.
